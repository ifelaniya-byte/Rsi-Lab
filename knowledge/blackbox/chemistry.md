FORMULAS: Alkene: CₙH₂ₙ; Alkyne: CₙH₂ₙ₋₂; addition: X₂ → vicinal dihalide.
REBUILD: Test with Br₂ (decolorization) or KMnO₄ (color change) to confirm unsaturation.

### CHEM-09-004
PRINCIPLE: Aromatic compounds possess delocalized π‑electron rings (e.g., benzene) giving extra stability.
FORMULAS: Hückel rule: 4n+2 π electrons; resonance energy ≈36 kcal/mol for benzene.
REBUILD: Identify by characteristic NMR (δ≈7‑8 ppm) and UV λmax ~200‑260 nm; resist addition reactions.

### CHEM-09-005
PRINCIPLE: Alcohols contain –OH group, enabling hydrogen bonding and polarity.
FORMULAS: R‑OH; acidity: pKa ≈15‑18; oxidation: RCH₂OH → RCHO → RCOOH.
REBUILD: Prepare via hydration of alkenes or reduction of carbonyls; confirm by IR O‑H stretch (~3400 cm⁻¹).

### CHEM-09-006
PRINCIPLE: Carboxylic acids bear –COOH, are acidic, and participate in ester/amide formation.
FORMULAS: R‑COOH; Ka ≈10⁻⁴‑10⁻⁵; esterification: RCOOH + R'OH ⇌ RCOOR' + H₂O.
REBUILD: Oxidize primary alcohols or hydrolyze nitriles; test with NaHCO₃ (effervescence).

### CHEM-09-007
PRINCIPLE: Amines are nitrogen‑containing bases; basicity varies with substitution.
FORMULAS: R₃N; Kb ≈10⁻³‑10⁻⁶; protonation: R₃N + H⁺ ⇌ R₃NH⁺.
REBUILD: Synthesize via alkylation of ammonia or reduction of nitro compounds; test pH of aqueous solution.

### CHEM-09-008
PRINCIPLE: Esters form from acid + alcohol, often fragrant; key to flavors and polyesters.
FORMULAS: RCOOH + R'OH ⇌ RCOOR' + H₂O (Fischer esterification); hydrolysis: RCOOR' + H₂O → RCOOH + R'OH.
REBUILD: Mix acid and alcohol with catalytic H₂SO₄, reflux, remove water; verify by IR C=O (~1735 cm⁻¹).

### CHEM-09-009
PRINCIPLE: Polymers are macromolecules built from repeating monomer units.
FORMULAS: Degree of polymerization DP = n; Mₙ = DP·M₀.
REBUILD: Purify monomer, initiate polymerization (radical, ionic, condensation), precipitate/purify polymer.

### CHEM-09-010
PRINCIPLE: Polymerization links monomers via covalent bonds to form chains.
FORMULAS: Chain‑growth: M• + M → M‑M•; Step‑growth: A‑B + A‑B → A‑B‑A‑B + by‑product.
REBUILD: Choose initiator (peroxide) or catalyst, control temperature, monitor conversion via viscosity or NMR.

### CHEM-09-011
PRINCIPLE: Substitution replaces one atom/group with another.
FORMULAS: SN1: R‑X → R⁺ + X⁻ → R‑Nu; SN2: Nu⁻ + R‑X → R‑Nu + X⁻ (concerted).
REBUILD: Conduct reaction in polar protic (SN1) or aprotic (SN2) solvent, monitor by TLC or GC.

### CHEM-09-012
PRINCIPLE: Addition adds reagents across multiple bonds (π bonds).
FORMULAS: Alkene + X₂ → X‑C‑C‑X; Markovnikov: H adds to carbon with more H.
REBUILD: Mix alkene with reagent (e.g., HBr) in inert solvent, observe product via IR/NMR.

### CHEM-09-013
PRINCIPLE: Elimination removes atoms/groups to form π bonds.
FORMULAS: E1: R‑CH₂‑CH₂‑X → R‑CH=CH₂ + HX (carbocation); E2: base abstracts H⁻, X⁻ leaves concurrently.
REBUILD: Treat alkyl halide with strong base (e.g., KOH) in ethanol, heat; detect alkene by bromine test.

### CHEM-09-014
PRINCIPLE: Isomers share molecular formula but differ in structure or spatial arrangement.
FORMULAS: Structural: different connectivity; Stereoisomers: enantiomers/diastereomers.
REBUILD: Separate via chromatography or crystallization; identify by NMR, IR, or optical rotation.

### CHEM-09-015
PRINCIPLE: Chirality arises from non‑superimposable mirror images (enantiomers).
FORMULAS: Chiral center: carbon with four different substituents; optical rotation [α] = Σ[α]i·ci.
REBUILD: Synthesize chiral molecule, measure optical activity with polarimeter; confirm by chiral HPLC or X‑ray.

### CHEM-10-002

FORMULAS: Rule of mixtures (approx): ρ_alloy = Σw_iρ_i; σ_y ≈ Σw_iσ_yi (weight fractions).

### CHEM-10-003

FORMULAS: SiO₂ + CaO → CaSiO₃ (example); no simple universal equation.

### CHEM-10-004

FORMULAS: SiO₂ network: Si–O–Si bonds; Tg ≈ 500‑900 °C depending on composition.

### CHEM-10-005

### CHEM-10-007

### CHEM-11-001

### CHEM-11-002

### CHEM-11-003
PRINCIPLE: Chromatography separates mixture components based on differential partitioning.
FORMULAS: Retention factor Rf = distance traveled by solute / distance traveled by solvent.

### CHEM-11-004
PRINCIPLE: NMR uses nuclear spin transitions in a magnetic field to elucidate molecular structure.

REBUILD: Dissolve sample in deuterated solvent, acquire ¹H and ¹³C spectra, interpret coupling and shifts.

### CHEM-11-005

FORMULAS: No universal equation; color correlates with electronic transitions (e.g., Na⁺ → yellow).

### CHEM-11-006

FORMULAS: Electrode: E = E₀ + (RT/F) ln a_H⁺; indicator: color change at pKa ±1.

### CHEM-11-008

FORMULAS: Propagation: σ_f = √( Σ(∂f/∂x_i·σ_x_i)² ); report value with appropriate sig figs.
