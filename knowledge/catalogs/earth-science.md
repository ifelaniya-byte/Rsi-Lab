# Teaching catalog: earth science

## 01 Geology
EARTH-01-001 | Rock cycle | Igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic transform | Use when reading Earth's history in stone
EARTH-01-002 | Plate tectonics | Crust plates drift on the mantle | Use when explaining earthquakes and continents
EARTH-01-003 | Earthquakes | Sudden fault slips release energy | Use when assessing seismic risk
EARTH-01-004 | Volcanoes | Magma reaches the surface | Use when living near or using volcanic land
EARTH-01-005 | Erosion | Wind, water, ice wear rock down | Use when managing land and coasts
EARTH-01-006 | Fossils in strata | Layered rocks date ancient life | Use when prospecting or dating terrain
EARTH-01-007 | Geological time | 4.5B years in eons and eras | Use as the deep-time framework
EARTH-01-008 | Faults and folds | Crust deformation structures | Use when mapping resources or hazards

## 02 Minerals and Mining
EARTH-02-001 | Mineral identification | Hardness, streak, cleavage, luster | Use when prospecting unknown deposits
EARTH-02-002 | Mohs hardness scale | 1 (talc) to 10 (diamond) | Use when testing mineral hardness in the field
EARTH-02-003 | Ores | Rocks worth extracting metal from | Use when restarting metallurgy
EARTH-02-004 | Iron ores | Hematite, magnetite; the key metals | Use as the first mining priority
EARTH-02-005 | Copper and tin | Bronze-age metals; easy to smelt | Use when bootstrapping metal tools
EARTH-02-006 | Prospecting | Reading land for mineral signs | Use when searching for new deposits
EARTH-02-007 | Open-pit vs shaft mining | Surface vs underground extraction | Use when planning any mine
EARTH-02-008 | Panning and sluicing | Water separates heavy minerals | Use for gold and placer deposits
EARTH-02-009 | Quarrying | Cutting building stone | Use when construction needs stone
EARTH-02-010 | Clay deposits | Source of pottery and bricks | Use when making ceramics

## 03 Metallurgy
EARTH-03-001 | Smelting | Heat plus carbon extracts metal from ore | Use as the core metal-making process
EARTH-03-002 | Charcoal making | Slow-burn wood into nearly pure carbon | Use as the smelter's fuel
EARTH-03-003 | Bloomery furnace | Early iron-smelting furnace | Use when bootstrapping iron production
EARTH-03-004 | Casting | Pouring molten metal into molds | Use when shaping tools and parts
EARTH-03-005 | Forging | Hammering hot metal into shape | Use when making strong tools and weapons
EARTH-03-006 | Annealing | Slow cooling softens worked metal | Use when metal gets brittle from working
EARTH-03-007 | Quenching and tempering | Hardening then toughening steel | Use when making blades and springs
EARTH-03-008 | Steel | Iron plus ~2% carbon; the master alloy | Use as the priority material to reach
EARTH-03-009 | Bronze | Copper plus tin; casts beautifully | Use as the easy early alloy
EARTH-03-010 | Soldering and brazing | Joining metals with filler | Use when assembling metal parts

## 04 Water
EARTH-04-001 | Finding water | Valleys, vegetation, animal signs | Use as the first survival skill
EARTH-04-002 | Wells | Digging to the water table | Use for permanent settlements
EARTH-04-003 | Boiling | Kills pathogens in water | Use as the simplest purification
EARTH-04-004 | Filtration | Sand and charcoal remove particles | Use when building water filters
EARTH-04-005 | Chlorination | Kills microbes chemically | Use for safe community water
EARTH-04-006 | Distillation | Boil and condense for pure water | Use when water is salty or contaminated
EARTH-04-007 | Rainwater harvesting | Catching and storing rain | Use in dry regions
EARTH-04-008 | Aqueducts | Gravity moves water long distances | Use when supplying cities
EARTH-04-009 | Irrigation | Controlled watering of crops | Use as the base of reliable agriculture
EARTH-04-010 | Water testing | Checking clarity, smell, and safety | Use before trusting any source

EARTH-05-001 | Atmospheric layers | Troposphere to exosphere | Use when understanding weather and flight
EARTH-05-002 | Weather vs climate | Daily conditions vs long-term patterns | Use when planning around seasons
EARTH-05-003 | Cloud reading | Cloud types predict coming weather | Use for short-term forecasting without instruments
EARTH-05-004 | Storms | Hurricanes, tornadoes, blizzards | Use when preparing shelter
EARTH-05-005 | Wind patterns | Global circulation cells | Use for sailing and siting windmills
EARTH-05-006 | Greenhouse effect | Gases trap heat; warming lever | Use when managing climate
EARTH-05-007 | Seasons | Tilt of Earth's axis | Use when timing agriculture
EARTH-05-008 | Barometers | Pressure predicts weather shifts | Use when building weather instruments

EARTH-06-002 | Soil texture | Sand, silt, clay proportions | Use when predicting drainage and workability
EARTH-06-003 | Composting | Rotting organic matter into fertilizer | Use when rebuilding soil fertility
EARTH-06-004 | Erosion control | Terracing, cover crops, windbreaks | Use when protecting farmland
EARTH-06-005 | Soil pH | Acidity affects nutrient uptake | Use when crops underperform
EARTH-06-006 | Nitrogen fixation | Legumes and bacteria add nitrogen | Use as free fertilizer
EARTH-06-007 | Manure | Animal waste as fertilizer | Use as the original soil amendment
EARTH-06-008 | Drainage | Removing excess water from fields | Use when land waterlogs
